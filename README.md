# MLB Statcast Project - Predicting xwOBA on Pitching Data and analyzing 2026 White Sox staff
Built an XGBoost model, inspired by FanGraphs' Stuff+, that predicts contact quality (xwOBA) from the physical characteristics of MLB pitches (2024-2026), then used it to evaluate the 2026 White Sox pitching staff.

Completed for Quant Methods course at the University of Notre Dame / Mendoza College of Business
